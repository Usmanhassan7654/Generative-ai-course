Marhaba Cattle & Dairy Farm - Staff Handbook

Staff record the milk from every cow in the morning and in the evening.

Morning milking starts at 5:00 AM and evening milking starts at 5:00 PM.

Rule SH-07: Every staff member must wash hands and clean the udder before milking.

Rule SH-14: If a milk record is wrong, staff must draw one line through the wrong number, write the correct number next to it, and ask the supervisor to sign the change.

Rule SH-15: Records must never be erased or torn out of the register.

The register is checked by the supervisor every Sunday.
