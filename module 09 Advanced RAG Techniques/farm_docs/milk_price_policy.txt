Marhaba Cattle & Dairy Farm - Milk Price Policy MILK-V2 (CURRENT, effective 1 November 2026)

Milk costs Rs 220 per litre.

This price applies only when the customer collects the milk at the farm gate.

Home delivery inside DHA and Clifton adds Rs 20 per litre to the price.

Customers who buy more than 20 litres in one order get Rs 10 off per litre.

Prices are reviewed every three months by the farm manager.
